# 01 — Brand Names, Taglines & Recommendation

## The 5 Finalists

### 1. Second Brain Is A Lie → **"Runway"**
**Full name:** Runway ADHD
**Tagline:** *Short runway, clear takeoff.*
**Why it works:** Evokes the short distance between intention and action — the core ADHD problem. Short, ownable, non-clinical, doesn't scream "productivity bro." Scales beyond ADHD later if you want.
**Risk:** "Runway" is heavily used in startup/finance SaaS. SEO contested.

### 2. **Dopamine Desk**
**Tagline:** *A workspace your brain actually wants to open.*
**Why it works:** Instant in-group recognition. "Dopamine" is native ADHD-community vocabulary (dopamine menu, dopamine seeking) and signals "we're one of you" in one word. Extremely strong on TikTok — the word itself is a hook.
**Risk:** Slightly cutesy; caps you at the consumer/community market. Harder to sell to a 42-year-old engineering manager.

### 3. **Low Friction**
**Tagline:** *Productivity with the hard parts taken out.*
**Why it works:** Names the actual mechanism of the product rather than the diagnosis. Memorable, calm, zero shame. Works for ADHD-adjacent buyers (burnout, long COVID, post-partum brain fog) which is a much larger market than diagnosed ADHD.
**Risk:** Doesn't self-identify as ADHD — needs the tagline/subhead to do that work. Weaker on ADHD-keyword search.

### 4. **Executive Reset**
**Tagline:** *Rebuild your executive function. Monthly.*
**Why it works:** The most premium-sounding option. "Executive function" is the clinically correct term and late-diagnosed adults know it intimately. Supports a $47→$147 price ladder and future B2B/coaching tiers.
**Risk:** Reads corporate at a glance ("executive" = C-suite). Slightly cold for a community product.

### 5. **The Brain Rental**
**Tagline:** *Borrow working executive function by the month.*
**Why it works:** The most differentiated name on this list. Honestly describes the business model (you are renting an external executive function, continuously maintained by someone else) and makes the subscription make intuitive sense — which is the #1 objection to recurring pricing on an "information" product.
**Risk:** Weird. Weird is good for organic reach and bad for cold paid traffic.

## ✅ Recommendation: **Dopamine Desk**

**Primary:** Dopamine Desk
**Tagline (use everywhere):** *A workspace your brain actually wants to open.*
**Sales-page subhead:** The ADHD operating system that updates every month — so you never abandon another planner again.

### Why this one

1. **Your acquisition channel is short-form video.** TikTok/Reels success is decided in 1.5 seconds. "Dopamine Desk" *is* a hook. "Executive Reset" and "Low Friction" require explanation; you cannot afford explanation at the top of a funnel.
2. **It signals in-group membership instantly.** Your core differentiator is "built by/for ADHD brains, not neurotypical advice." The name has to carry that before a word of copy is read. It does.
3. **It is a noun you can show.** A "desk" is a visual object — perfect for screen-recording the Notion workspace, perfect for merch/spin-offs later, perfect for "open your Dopamine Desk" as a daily behavioural cue inside the product.
4. **Domain/handle availability is realistically better** than Runway or Executive Reset, both of which are crowded.

### Guardrails
- Register `dopaminedesk.com` + `@dopaminedesk` on TikTok, IG, YouTube, Reddit, X **before** publishing anything.
- Do not over-use "dopamine" in body copy — once in the name is plenty. Overuse reads as pop-neuroscience and erodes trust with the late-diagnosed, well-read segment.
- Keep **"Executive Reset"** in your back pocket as the name of the Tier-3 high-ticket program ($97–147/mo small group). It is the perfect premium sub-brand, and it means the monthly live call ("System Reset") already matches.

## Brand voice rules (hand these to every writer/AI prompt)

**We sound like:** a friend who has read the research, has the same brain, and has no interest in making you feel bad.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Here's the version for a bad day." | "Here's how to stay consistent." |
| "You didn't fail. The tool required a brain you don't have." | "You just need the right system!" |
| "Took me 4 restarts." | "I finally cracked it." |
| Short sentences. Concrete nouns. | Hustle language, "crush," "dominate," "10x." |
| Name the friction out loud. | Pretend the product is effortless. |
| "Not medical advice. Not a treatment." | Any claim about symptoms, medication, or diagnosis. |

**Three words we never use:** *discipline, lazy, just.*
