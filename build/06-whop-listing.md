## Long description (paste into Whop product page)

**You've already bought the planner. This isn't that.**

Dopamine Desk is a living ADHD operating system in Notion — plus a room full of people who actually get it.

It's built around four things every other productivity tool gets wrong for our brains:

**1. It's sorted by energy, not by time.** Your tasks are tagged by how much they'll cost you (low / medium / high) and what kind of reward they give. Open it on a bad day, tap "low energy," get three things you can actually do. Time blindness means a clock-based system will fail you; an energy-based one won't.

**2. It has a bad-day mode.** One button strips the whole workspace down to three survivable actions and a reminder that maintenance days are a legitimate category of day. No counts, no streaks, no overdue red.

**3. It expects you to disappear.** There's a Re-Entry page: four steps, under five minutes, for coming back after a week or a month away. No shame copy, no streak to mourn, nothing to catch up on. Members average two to four absences a year and the system is designed around that fact.

**4. Somebody else maintains it.** This is the real difference. Every month, one or two new modules or upgrades ship — based on what members actually asked for. Your system gets better while you're not looking. That's what the subscription is: you're not renting files, you're renting the maintenance you were never going to do.

### What's inside The Desk
Today / One Thing view · frictionless Brain Dump (voice-to-text friendly) · energy-based task manager · time-blindness tools with a personal estimate multiplier · 12 modular routines, each with a 3-step bad-day minimum · Project Decomposer · the 18-minute weekly review · an imperfect-streak habit tracker that counts 4-out-of-7 as a win · a 25-piece Resource Vault (scripts for hard conversations, the doom-pile checklist, the 2pm Crash Protocol, the "I've been avoiding this email for 9 days" script) · Bad Day Mode · Re-Entry.

### What's inside The Crew
Live **Open Desk** body-doubling sessions several times a week, cameras optional, no small talk required · **The Reset**, a monthly live call where we rebuild whatever broke · #low-energy-days, the most-used channel in the community and the reason people stay · accountability pairing, as light or heavy as you want · priority requests, which means you ask for a module and it gets built.

### Who this is for
Adults with ADHD — diagnosed, self-identified, waitlisted, or told at 34 that it was "just anxiety." Freelancers, remote workers, parents, students, knowledge workers. Especially if you've abandoned three or more systems and have started to assume the problem is you.

### Who this isn't for
Anyone who wants a one-time template (you already own four). Anyone looking for therapy, coaching, or medical guidance — we don't do any of those and we'll say so. Anyone who wants a system that requires a four-hour setup day to work.

**Instant access. Cancel in two clicks. 14-day guarantee: email the word *refund* and that's the whole process.**

*Not medical advice. Not a treatment for ADHD. Systems and company.*

## FAQ (paste into Whop FAQ section)

**Do I need to know Notion?**
No. You duplicate one link and you're in. There's a 4-minute first-run page that gets you to one captured thought and one chosen task, and explicitly tells you not to set anything else up today. If you can use Notes, you can use this.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes — it's designed phone-first. Every view is built for portrait mode, logging your energy is one tap, and capturing a thought is voice-to-text with no fields to fill in. Most members use it 80% on mobile.

**Do I need a paid Notion account?**
No. The free Notion plan is plenty for everything here.

**I'm not diagnosed. Can I join?**
Yes, and nobody will ask. Self-identified, on a waiting list, assessed and dismissed, or diagnosed at 38 — same room, same system.

**What's actually new each month?**
One or two modules or significant upgrades, driven by member requests, logged in a public Changelog with the requester's name on it. Recent examples of the kind of thing: a medication-day tracker, a "returning from leave" protocol, a hyperfocus exit routine. You can see the whole history before you buy.

**Will the updates break my setup?**
No. New modules ship as standalone pages you drag into your own Desk, so your existing data is never touched. Core database properties get added to, never renamed or deleted.

**What if I stop using it for a month?**
Then you'll use the Re-Entry page, which exists precisely for that, takes under five minutes, and involves deleting things rather than catching up. Nobody in the community will comment on your absence — that's an actual rule, not a vibe.

**What are the body-doubling sessions like?**
A video call where everyone says one sentence about what they're working on, then mutes and works. Cameras optional. No small talk, no networking, no check-out round if you need to leave early. Sessions run multiple times a week, including at least one in the evening and one at a weekend.

**Is this coaching or therapy?**
No. It's systems and community. We don't give medical advice, don't discuss medication protocols, and aren't clinicians. Plenty of members use this alongside therapy or medication; it's not a substitute for either.

**Can I just buy the template once?**
Deliberately not. A static ADHD template is the exact thing that's already failed you several times — the maintenance assumption is what kills it. The subscription *is* the maintenance.

**Tier 1 or Tier 2?**
Tier 2 unless money is genuinely tight. The system is what you buy; the community is what makes you use it. Crew members are far more likely to still be active at 90 days, and body-doubling is the single highest-impact thing in the product.

**How do I cancel?**
Two clicks in your Whop account. No call, no email, no retention form, no "are you sure" maze. And if you're within 14 days, email the word *refund* and you'll get your money back without being asked why.

**Who's behind this?**
