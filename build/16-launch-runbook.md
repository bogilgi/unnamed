# 16 — The Launch Runbook

Sixteen documents is itself a doom pile. This is the single ordered path through them.

**Rules for using this:**
- **Do the phases in order** — note that Phase 4 (the free front door) comes before Phase 1 (the build). Each phase unblocks the next. The one unrecoverable mistake is opening a Whop store that points at an empty Notion template.
- **One session = one sitting.** Nothing here is longer than ~3 hours.
- **The "not yet" list at the bottom is load-bearing.** Everything on it will feel urgent and all of it is a trap before you have 20 members.
- Total: **~28 hours of work** across 4–6 weeks at a realistic pace, and **$0 up front** (see doc 17).

## NOT YET — the list that protects you

Every one of these will feel urgent. All of them are traps before 20 paying members.

| Don't | Until |
|---|---|
| Paid ads | You have organic proof and a known churn number |
| Affiliate program | 20 members, and recruit only from happy ones |
| Tier 3 (`Executive Reset`) | 100 Tier-2 members **and** 3 unsolicited asks for 1:1 help |
| A second content channel | One channel is working and consistent |
| More Notion modules | Members have asked for something specific |
| Whop marketplace listing | You have 20 members and real reviews |
| A redesign of anything | A member has told you it's confusing |
| Hiring any help | You are personally at capacity, not just tired |

## The five numbers (doc 09)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| MRR | $1k → $5k → $11k |
| Monthly churn | under 8% after month 3 |
| Day-30 active rate | over 60% |
| Open Desk attendance | over 25% of Crew, monthly |
| Modules shipped on time | **12 of 12 per year** |

**If churn goes above 12%:** stop all content and all new features. The problem is onboarding or community, never module count. Interview ten members instead.

---

## Honest expectations

- **First paying member:** achievable in week one of posting, and it will probably be someone who already knows you.
- **First 20:** 1–3 months of daily posting. This is the hard part and it's where most people quit.
- **$5k MRR (~105 members):** realistically 6–12 months with consistent organic content.
- **The thing that kills this business:** not competition, not pricing, not the product. Breaking the monthly update promise twice. Protect those ten hours a month ahead of everything else on this page.
