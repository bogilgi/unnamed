# 17 — Launching for $0: Every Step

Nothing in this document costs money up front. The only money that ever leaves is Whop's cut of a sale you've already made.

**One caveat before you start:** every dashboard below changes its button labels every few months. I've given the real structure and the thing to look for, not a promise about wording. If a label doesn't match, look for the nearest equivalent rather than assuming you're in the wrong place.

---

## The stack, and what each one costs

| Need | Tool | Cost | Limit |

| The product | **Notion** free plan | $0 | Unlimited for one person |
| Community | **Discord** | $0 | None that matter |

**Not needed:** a domain, a website builder, Zoom (its free tier cuts you off at 40 minutes, which is exactly when body doubling gets useful — Discord voice is unlimited), Loom, a Notion paid plan, or any course platform.

---

## What Whop actually takes

Set up and listing are free; Whop earns per sale. As of late 2026, third-party reviews consistently report:

- **~2.7% + $0.30** per sale on domestic cards
- **+1.5%** on international cards
- **ACH** around 1.5%, capped at ~$5
- **Possibly +3%** on sales gated through a Whop automation such as Discord. This one is disputed — some sources say it isn't on Whop's published pricing — so **check the Discord app's own listing inside Whop before you rely on either number.**
- **Payouts** may carry a small fee depending on method

On a $47 sale that's roughly **$1.60–$3.00**, so you keep about $44–45. Verify the current numbers on Whop's pricing page at setup; don't take a figure in this document as current.

> **The alternative worth knowing about:** Whop has its own built-in community apps (Forums, Chat, Courses, Events). Running the community *inside* Whop means one platform instead of two and sidesteps the Discord-automation fee question entirely.
>
> **I'd still use Discord**, for two reasons: body doubling needs good unlimited voice, and your audience already lives there. The possible 3% is about $1.40 on a $47 sale — not worth restructuring the product over. But if the two-platform setup is what stops you shipping, Whop-only is a legitimate choice and you can add Discord later.

# DAY 1 — 30 minutes

## 1. Claim the handles (15 min)

**Check one string on all four platforms before you claim any of them.** Claiming piecemeal is how you end up as `@dopaminedesk` on TikTok, `@dopaminedesk.co` on Instagram and `@dopaminedeskhq` on YouTube — which costs real follows, because people search the name they saw and find nothing.

Check in this order and take the first string that's free on **TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Reddit** together:

1. `dopaminedesk`
2. `thedopaminedesk`
3. `dopaminedesks`
4. `dopaminedeskhq`

Consistency beats elegance — take the one that's available everywhere even if it isn't your favourite. Then claim all four, including platforms you don't plan to post to; reclaiming a taken handle is impossible, and this is the only irreversible item in the whole document.

> Gmail availability tells you nothing here. That namespace has been saturated for fifteen years and almost nobody sees your address anyway — it's your reply-to and refund address, not a public identity.

## 1b. The identity sheet — what to put in every field

Fill these in once and keep them identical everywhere. Replace the handle with whatever you actually landed on.

| Platform | Username / handle | Name field | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gmail | `dopaminedeskadhd` | First: `Dopamine` · Last: `Desk` | Shows as "Dopamine Desk". **Never** add "Support" or "Team" — it reads like a ticket queue, and the whole positioning is a friend with the same brain |
| Beehiiv sender | — | **your first name** | Email from a person gets opened far more than email from a brand. Reply-to stays the Gmail |
| TikTok | `dopaminedeskadhd` | `Dopamine Desk · ADHD systems` | See the keyword note below |
| Instagram | `dopaminedeskadhd` | `Dopamine Desk \| ADHD systems` | Same |
| YouTube | `@dopaminedeskadhd` | `Dopamine Desk` | Channel name only |
| Reddit | `dopaminedeskadhd` | leave blank | See the Reddit note below |
| Whop | `dopamine-desk` | `Dopamine Desk` | |
| Discord server | — | `Dopamine Desk` | Your own nickname: `[your name] (founder)` |

**The Name field on TikTok and Instagram is searchable; the username is not.** Don't waste it repeating your handle — put the words people actually type, i.e. "ADHD systems". That's free discovery daily, and most accounts throw it away on a duplicate of their @.

**Don't present as a brand on Reddit.** r/ADHD and r/adhdwomen downvote and often remove accounts that read as products. Leave the display name empty, build genuine comment history for a week or two before posting anything, and keep your link in your profile only — never in a post body. Reddit is the one platform where looking like a business is a liability.

### Bios

TikTok / Instagram:
```
ADHD systems that survive a bad week
Built by someone with the same brain
Free 20-min reset ↓
```

YouTube:
```
Productivity systems for brains that abandoned four planners.
Energy-based planning, body doubling, and no productivity shame.
```

Reddit profile — one plain line, no pitch:
```
Late-diagnosed. I build ADHD systems.
```

### A note on Gmail addresses
Gmail ignores dots, so `dopamine.desk@gmail.com` is the same account as `dopaminedesk@gmail.com` — if one is taken, every dotted variant is too. You need a genuinely different string. Best to worst: `dopaminedeskadhd`, `dopaminedesks`, `hellodopaminedesk`, `dopaminedeskhq`, `getdopaminedesk`, `dopaminedeskco`. Avoid anything with a year in it; it looks temporary and dates the account.

**Settled:** `dopaminedeskadhd@gmail.com`. The social handles should match it — `dopaminedeskadhd` on all four platforms.

## 2. Make the email address (2 min)
`dopaminedeskadhd@gmail.com` — done. Keep it entirely separate from your personal inbox. This is your refund address, your support address and your reply-to, and it does not need to match your social handles.

## 3. Beehiiv account (5 min)
Sign up free at beehiiv.com. Name the publication **Dopamine Desk**. It'll give you `dopaminedesk.beehiiv.com` — that's your landing-page URL, and it's enough to launch with. Don't configure anything else yet.

## 4. Whop account (8 min)
Go to **whop.com/sell**. Onboarding asks you to name your whop, pick a niche and choose a custom URL — all changeable later, so don't deliberate. Then find the payout/banking section and **start verification now**: it takes days, and you want it done before you have something to sell.

**Stop here for today.** You've claimed everything that can be taken and started the only clock you don't control.

# DAY 2 — The front door (2 hours)

This is Phase 4 of the runbook, and it comes before building the product. The front door is cheap; the product is expensive. Find out whether anyone wants it first.

## 1. Build the 20-Minute Reset in Notion (45 min)
- New Notion page, title **The 20-Minute Reset**
- Paste the body from **doc 05** (everything from "Read this first" to the end of Step 5)
- Keep the checkboxes as real Notion to-dos and the fill-in blocks as code blocks — people duplicate and type into them
- Top right → **Share** → **Publish** → turn on **Allow duplicate as template**
- Copy the public link
- **Test it in a logged-out browser.** If it doesn't duplicate for a stranger, nothing else works.

## 2. Build the opt-in page in Beehiiv (45 min)
In Beehiiv, find the website/landing-page builder:
- Headline: **The 20-Minute Reset**
- Body: the opt-in copy from doc 05 ("A single page you fill in once, when everything has piled up…")
- Button: **Send it to me**
- Turn off anything about "latest posts" or an archive — you have no posts, and an empty archive reads as abandoned
- Publish. Note the URL.

## 3. Load the four emails (20 min)
Beehiiv → automations. Create one triggered by a new subscriber, with the four emails from doc 05:
- **Email 1** — immediately, subject `your 20-Minute Reset, as promised`, containing the Notion link
- **Email 2** — day 2, `did you have a fire?`
- **Email 3** — day 4, `why your planner abandoned you`
- **Email 4** — day 7, `the 2pm problem`

Set the sender name to your name, reply-to `dopaminedeskadhd@gmail.com`. Turn the automation on.

## 4. Test the whole funnel (10 min)
Subscribe with a different email address of your own. Confirm: email 1 arrives, link works, page duplicates. Then check the sender name looks like a person, not a company.

**Done:** you have a working free front door. Put the URL in all four bios today.

# DAYS 3–21 — Build the Desk, post daily

Two tracks in parallel. This is the longest stretch and the one where projects die.

**Track A — build (~13 h):** Phases 1–3 of the runbook. Prompts in doc 04, spec in doc 03, data in `notion-csv/`, Vault content in docs 10–13.

**Track B — post (~30 min/day):** doc 07's calendar. First video goes up **within two weeks of today**, finished product or not. Non-negotiable, because the build will otherwise expand to fill every available week.

> Book a free body-doubling session for your first build sitting — a "study with me" stream will do. You've written a product whose central claim is that you can't start alone and shouldn't have to. Don't make the build the exception.

# DAY ~22 — Discord (1 hour, $0)

## 1. Create the server (10 min)
Discord → **+** → Create My Own → For a club or community. Name it **Dopamine Desk**.

## 2. Create the channels (15 min)
Nine, in this order — put `#low-energy-days` high up, because it'll be the most-used:
```
#welcome-and-rules      #introduce-yourself     #wins-and-progress
#low-energy-days        #system-help            #accountability
#body-doubling          #resources-and-shares   #off-topic
```
Plus **two voice channels**: `🔊 Open Desk` and `🔊 Dread Hour`.

## 3. Create the roles (10 min)
Server Settings → Roles → create `desk` and `crew`.
- `desk`: can **view** every channel, can **send messages** only in `#system-help`
- `crew`: can send messages everywhere
- Then make every channel private to those two roles, so nothing is visible to non-members

## 4. Pin the posts (15 min)
From **doc 08**: the rules post in `#welcome-and-rules`, the intro prompt, the `#low-energy-days` etiquette post, the wins prompt. Pin each one.

## 5. Schedule four weeks of sessions (10 min)
Server Settings → Events. Create recurring events matching `notion-csv/07-sessions-4weeks.csv`: Monday 09:30 Open Desk, Wednesday 19:00 Dread Hour, Friday 11:00 Admin Sprint, Saturday 10:00 Open Desk. **Include one evening and one weekend slot** — otherwise you've built it only for people with your schedule.

# DAY ~23 — Whop (1 hour, $0)

Only do this once the Desk is finished and the Vault is filled. A store pointing at an empty template is the one mistake you can't take back with the first fifty people who find you.

## 1. Set up the store (10 min)
In your Whop dashboard: logo (1:1), banner (3:1), short description and the long description from **doc 06**. Upload five gallery images: mobile dashboard, energy board, Bad Day Mode, Changelog, a Vault script. Add the 90-second walkthrough video.

## 2. Create product one — `Desk` (10 min)
Dashboard → **Products** → **Create product**. Name it `Desk`. Add two pricing options:
- $29 / month recurring
- $249 / year recurring

Whop supports multiple pricing options on one product, which is how you offer monthly and annual without duplicating anything. **Do not add a free trial.**

## 3. Create product two — `Desk + Crew` (10 min)
Same again: $47/month and $397/year. Mark this one **featured** — it's your main offer and should be visually dominant.

## 4. Connect Discord (10 min)
Find the Discord app/integration in Whop, authorise it on your server, and map:
- `Desk` → role `desk`
- `Desk + Crew` → role `crew`

**While you're on that page, read its stated fees.** This is where the extra-percentage question gets settled, and it'll be accurate because it's Whop's own listing.

## 5. Gate the Notion link (10 min)
Create the member-facing content inside each product so the Notion duplicate link is only visible after purchase. **Never paste that link in Discord** — a single screenshot and your product is free forever.

## 6. Founding-member code (5 min)
Promo code `FOUNDING50`: $37/month, 50 redemptions, and **say in the listing that the price is locked for life**. Then honour it forever, including when it's annoying.

## 7. Test it properly (15 min)
With a second account and a real card:
- Buy `Desk + Crew` → does the `crew` role land? Is the Notion link visible?
- Duplicate the Notion template as that member
- Cancel → is the role removed?
- Refund yourself from the dashboard
- Email `dopaminedeskadhd@gmail.com` the word *refund* and confirm it arrives somewhere you'll actually see it

**Done when you've been through buy → access → cancel → refund yourself.** Nobody else should discover a broken step in that chain.

## What costs money later, and when it's worth it

| Thing | Cost | Buy it when |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | ~$12/yr | You have paying members |
| Beehiiv paid | from ~$39/mo | You pass 2,500 subscribers |
| Canva Pro | ~$13/mo | Never, probably |
| A video editor | varies | You're posting daily and editing is the bottleneck |
| Help with the community | — | You're genuinely at capacity, not just tired |

**Nothing on this list is needed to get your first hundred members.** If you find yourself buying tools before you've posted thirty videos, that's the planning-instead-of-shipping failure mode with a receipt attached.
